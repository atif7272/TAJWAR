✦ FOR THE SISTER OF MY HEART

Minahil — Eid Mubarak! As the crescent of Shawwal rises, my first thought is to thank Allah for having you in my life. You’re not just a friend; you are the sister I never knew I needed. From the very first day, you treated me like your own brother — with kindness that felt like home.

Whether it was calming my anxious thoughts, staying up to help me study, or simply listening with your gentle heart — you’ve been my anchor. The way you say “Ati” with such warmth makes even hard days softer. Today, on Eid, I want to honour that kindness.

You help me emotionally, academically, and you never let me feel alone. How could I ever put that into words?

✦ FOR EVERY LATE NIGHT & GUIDING WORD

There were moments when I felt lost — in studies, in emotions, in life. And every time, you were there, Minahil. You’d check on me, share your notes, give me the most sincere advice, and remind me that I’m capable. You never judged, never made me feel small. Instead, you lifted me.

You call me “Ati” — and that name on your lips feels like a blessing. I still remember how you patiently explained concepts I couldn't grasp, or sent me duas when I was stressed. Your politeness and grace are unmatched. Allah has truly gifted me a sister in you.

May this Eid be a reward for every selfless act you’ve done. You deserve all the happiness, success, and peace that this world can offer — and more.

✦ MY PRAYERS FOR YOU — MY SISTER
اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لِمِينَاہِلَ فِي عِلْمِهَا وَعَمَلِهَا
O Allah, bless Minahil in her knowledge and her deeds — make her studies a source of barakah and grant her excellence in every exam.
اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْهَا كَمَا تَحْفَظُ الصَّالِحَاتِ
O Allah, protect her as You protect the righteous. Surround her with peace, keep her heart content, and reward her kindness manifold.
اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْهَا سَعَادَةً دَائِمَةً وَرِزْقًا حَلَالًا
O Allah, grant her everlasting happiness, pure provision, and a life filled with beautiful moments. Let her always be surrounded by those who cherish her.
جَزَاكِ اللَّهُ خَيْرًا كَثِيرًا يَا أُخْتِي
May Allah reward you with abundant goodness, my sister. Thank you for every time you held my hand, guided me, and treated me like family.
✦ A BROTHER'S GRATITUDE

Minahil, you've shown me what it means to have a sister. You never hesitated to help me — whether I was struggling with studies or needed emotional support. Your politeness, your patient way of explaining things, your kindness without expectation — it’s rare, and it’s precious.

You call me “Ati” like a real sister would, and that melts my heart. In a world where many keep to themselves, you chose to be my support system. I pray that Allah grants you the highest ranks in this life and the next, that your dreams unfold like the petals of a rose, and that every time you need someone, you find love and care.

Eid Mubarak, dear sister. Thank you for being my strength, my teacher, and my family. May our bond always grow in goodness, and may Allah accept all your duas.

— With eternal gratitude, your brother, Ati
